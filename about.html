About the Firm

Feigen Advisors is a pure-play CEO advisory firm, retained exclusively by chief executives of the world’s leading enterprises.

Founded in 2007 by Marc Feigen, the firm works with roughly a dozen active CEO clients whose companies represent close to a trillion dollars in combined market value.

We sit on the CEO’s side of the table: independent, discreet, and accountable only to the chief executive.

Our Practice

CEO Counsel. Trusted, confidential advice to the chief executive across the full arc of the role.

CEO Succession. Guidance for boards and incoming chief executives through transition and the first years in office.

CEO Performance. Helping sitting CEOs lead at their best, from strategy and capital allocation to the board relationship.

Our Values

Retained by the CEO. On the CEO’s side of the table. Measured by the long-term performance of the leaders we serve.
